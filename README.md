# The plan make.fun will carry out in public

A program that writes a 280-character plan for turning $100 into the most money in 30 days.

The plan: sell finished 15-second vertical video ads to local gyms and salons that have no video ads. Day one, find 30 of them on Google Maps; build each a finished ad from their own photos and reviews with ffmpeg and TTS; email a watermarked sample free; sell the clean pack for $49 in USDC. The $100 covers a domain and an email tool. Realistic expectation: 8 buyers = $392. The edge: buyers see the finished ad before paying.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the plan to `/output/entry.txt` (1–280 characters, UTF-8), after validating the length and that the plan names the $49 price and the $100 budget.
